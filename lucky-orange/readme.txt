== Description ==
## Lucky Orange: see why visitors leave your WordPress site, and what to fix first
Lucky Orange is a website behavior analytics plugin for WordPress. It adds heatmaps, session recordings, conversion funnels, form analytics, live chat, surveys, and Discovery AI to your site with one install. Over 500,000 websites use Lucky Orange to find where visitors get stuck and turn more traffic into sales and leads.

Google Analytics shows you what happened on your site. Lucky Orange shows you why.

The plugin adds the Lucky Orange tracking code to every page of your site. The script loads asynchronously, so it does not slow down your pages. You view and analyze your data in the Lucky Orange dashboard.

## Features
**Discovery AI**
Ask a question about your site in plain language. Discovery AI reads your live visitor behavior and answers with links to the heatmaps, recordings, or funnels that prove it. Use Quick Actions for one-click audits, or Heatmap Analyze to flag friction on any page.

**Discovery Visualize**
See the answer, not just read it. Discovery Visualize puts your questions, Discovery AI's written analysis, and charts in one workspace. Ask how your site performed this week, and it builds charts for session trends, devices, and traffic sources next to its findings. Use Focus Mode to ask follow-up questions about any chart, arrange cards your way, and download the workspace or a single chart as a PNG to share with your team.

**Dynamic Heatmaps**
Click, scroll, and move maps for every page, including dropdowns, popups, and other dynamic elements that many heatmap tools miss. Works with single-page apps and AJAX-loaded content.

**Session Recordings**
Replay real visitor sessions to see where people hesitate, get confused, or leave. Filter recordings to the visitors who matter, such as those who abandoned a cart or a form.

**Conversion Funnels**
Map each step of your checkout or signup flow and see exactly where visitors drop off.

**Form Analytics**
See which form fields slow visitors down and where they abandon your forms.

**Page Insights**
Get key stats for any page: top-clicked elements, frustration signals, and engagement trends.

**Visitor Profiles**
See each visitor's full history in one view, including traffic source and every session they've had.

**Live View**
Watch what visitors are doing on your site right now.

**Live Chat**
Talk to visitors in real time. Trigger chat invites when visitors stall or rage-click, and see their session context while you chat.

**Surveys and Announcements**
Ask visitors what they're looking for or why they didn't buy. Show targeted messages and offers by device, behavior, or traffic source. Unlimited on every plan.

== Installation ==

1. In your WordPress admin, go to Plugins > Add New and search for "Lucky Orange."
2. Click Install Now, then Activate.
3. Open the Lucky Orange plugin settings and sign in, or start your free trial.
4. The plugin adds the tracking code to every page automatically. No code editing needed.
5. Visit your site, then open Live View in your Lucky Orange dashboard to see your visit come in.

**Using a caching plugin?** Clear your cache after activating so the tracking code appears on cached pages.

**Prefer Google Tag Manager?** You can install Lucky Orange through [Google Tag Manager](https://www.luckyorange.com/integrations/gtm) instead of this plugin.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= What does the Lucky Orange WordPress plugin do? =
It adds the Lucky Orange tracking code to every page of your WordPress site. That turns on heatmaps, session recordings, conversion funnels, form analytics, live chat, surveys, and Discovery AI. You view your data in the Lucky Orange dashboard.

= Is Lucky Orange free? =
Lucky Orange offers a 7-day free trial with full access to every feature, including Discovery AI. After the trial, paid plans start at $32/mo, billed annually. A free plan with 100 sessions per month is also available.

= Will Lucky Orange slow down my WordPress site? =
No. The Lucky Orange script loads asynchronously, so your pages load and render without waiting for it.

= Does Lucky Orange work with WooCommerce? =
Yes. Lucky Orange works on WooCommerce stores and any other WordPress site. Use funnels and session recordings to see where shoppers drop off between product page, cart, and checkout. The plugin does not import WooCommerce order data.

= Does Lucky Orange work with Elementor, Divi, or other page builders? =
Yes. The plugin adds the tracking code site-wide, so it works with any theme or page builder. Dynamic Heatmaps also capture clicks inside popups, dropdowns, and other dynamic elements.

= Does Lucky Orange work with caching plugins? =
Yes. The plugin is built to work with caching plugins. After activating it, clear your cache so the tracking code appears on cached pages.

= Can Lucky Orange adhere to GDPR regulations? =
Yes. Lucky Orange can adhere to GDPR regulations using its built-in privacy tools. These include a consent API that waits for visitor opt-in, default keystroke masking, IP address settings, Do Not Track support, and a Data Processing Addendum. See the [Privacy and Security help center](https://help.luckyorange.com/category/255-privacy-security) for details.

= Does Lucky Orange record passwords or credit card numbers? =
No. Passwords, credit card numbers, and Social Security numbers are never recorded. All other keystrokes are masked as asterisks by default, and you can hide any page element with a CSS class.

= What is Discovery AI? =
Discovery AI is Lucky Orange's built-in AI analyst. Ask a question about your site in plain language, and it answers using your live visitor behavior, with links to the heatmaps, recordings, or funnels behind the answer. It's included in the free trial and available as an add-on on paid plans.

= What is Discovery Visualize? =
Discovery Visualize is a workspace inside Discovery AI that pairs written analysis with charts built from your live visitor data, such as session trends and device or traffic source breakdowns. You can ask follow-up questions about any chart with Focus Mode and download charts as PNGs to share.

= How is Lucky Orange different from Google Analytics? =
Google Analytics shows what happened on your site, such as traffic and bounce rate. Lucky Orange shows why, with heatmaps, session recordings, and form analytics that reveal what visitors actually did. Many teams use both, and Lucky Orange integrates with GA4.

= Is Lucky Orange a good Hotjar alternative? =
Yes. Lucky Orange offers heatmaps, session recordings, surveys, and feedback tools like Hotjar. Unlike Hotjar, every Lucky Orange plan includes every tool, including live chat and surveys, at no extra cost. Lucky Orange also adds Discovery AI, conversion funnels, and form analytics.

= Can agencies use Lucky Orange on client sites? =
Yes. Every plan includes unlimited team members, and you can add more websites for $5 per site.

= Where do I see my data? =
In the Lucky Orange dashboard at app.luckyorange.com. The plugin handles setup on your WordPress site, and all analysis happens in the dashboard.

= How long does Lucky Orange keep my data? =
Data is stored for 60 days on every plan. You can upgrade storage to 90, 180, or 365 days.
